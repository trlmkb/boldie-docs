title: "What Boldie does not do yet"
description: "What Boldie does not do yet."
---

So that nobody is surprised:

- **Salon setup screens:** creating a salon, staff columns, working hours, days off and closures are done by the
  Boldie team during onboarding.
- **Recurring appointments** (standing weekly or monthly bookings). Repeating *blocks* exist.
- **Lithuanian public holidays** are not added automatically; closures are set up as date exceptions.
- **Lithuanian staff screens:** the admin and the staff apps are in English; client-facing pages and messages are
  Lithuanian and English.
- **A column per chair or room** in the calendar. Chairs and rooms are respected when booking, but not drawn.
- **Client payments:** deposits, card on file, no-show fees and gift vouchers are planned for a later version. Boldie
  billing is only the salon's own subscription.
- **Two-way sync with Google or Outlook.** The calendar subscription is one-way.
- **Report export**, **password reset**, Latvian and Estonian.

## A Lithuanian version of this guide

This guide is in English. A Lithuanian version is planned once the staff screens have Lithuanian wording, so the guide
and the screens use the same words. The screenshots of client-facing pages already show the Lithuanian booking flow.
