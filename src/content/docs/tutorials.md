## A. Book a colour and use the processing time

Asta comes at 10:00 for a full colour. Her colour develops for 45 minutes. Tomas wants a haircut at 10:30 with the same
stylist.

1. Make sure **Full colour** has a **Processing** segment with **Staff busy** unticked ([Services and resources](/services-and-resources/#services)).
2. Book Asta at 10:00 with Rūta. The calendar draws Active, then a dashed **processing** outline, then Finishing.
3. Click inside the processing outline in Rūta's column and book Tomas's haircut there.
4. Boldie accepts it because Rūta is free while Asta's colour develops, and Asta keeps her chair because the processing
   segment **Needs** a chair.

The day view in [The calendar](/calendar/) shows exactly this.

## B. Your first morning

1. Open **Calendar**. Check the marks: ✓ means confirmed, red ! means the reminder failed (phone that client), × means
   they asked to cancel (phone them).
2. Open the bell to see overnight online bookings and replies.
3. Open **Online → Requests** if you approve bookings.
4. When a client walks in, open their booking and press **Arrived**. Their stylist gets a "Client arrived" alert on
   the phone.
5. At the end of the visit, press **Done** and add a formula note.

## C. Put online booking on your website

1. **Settings → Online booking:** tick **Clients can book online**, choose the page address, set the rules, **Save
   online booking**.
2. **Services online:** untick anything that needs a consultation first. Limit long services to the days that suit
   you.
3. **Staff online:** untick anyone who does not take online clients.
4. Press **Open booking page** and book a test visit with your own phone.
5. **Copy script tag** and paste it into your website, or share the page address on Instagram and Google.

## D. Move from Treatwell or Fresha

1. Export your client list from Treatwell or Fresha (Fresha: Clients → Options → Export).
2. **Import & export:** choose **Treatwell export** or **Fresha export**, upload, check the column matches, tag the
   imported clients (for example "treatwell"), **Preview**, import.
3. Or press **Send it to us** and the Boldie team imports it for you within the 14-day support access.
4. Run **Clients → Find duplicates** if you imported from more than one place.
5. Enter future appointments in the Boldie calendar, then turn on reminders and online booking.

Treatwell's client export does not include visit history. Keep your old account open read-only for as long as you need
the history.

## E. Fill quiet weekdays with a campaign

1. **Marketing → Campaigns → New campaign**, name it "November colour".
2. Filter **Services visited: Full colour**, **Last visit** more than 8 weeks ago, **Only clients with no upcoming
   booking**.
3. Write the Lithuanian text with the booking link, keep {optout}.
4. Check the audience panel: who agreed, who the cap stops, the SMS parts estimate.
5. **Send later** at 10:00 on Tuesday. Afterwards, check **Booked within 14 days**.

## F. A client replies "2" to a reminder

If there is still time before your "online changes until" deadline (24 hours by default), Boldie cancels the booking
for you, answers the client, and offers the freed time to the waitlist. You get a "Client cancelled" alert.

If the reply comes later than that, Boldie does not cancel:

1. The booking gets the × mark and staff get a "Client asked to cancel" alert.
2. Phone the client. If they cancel, open the booking, **Cancel booking**, tick **Client cancelled**, **Confirm
   cancellation**.
3. The freed time goes to the waitlist automatically if someone is waiting for it.

## G. A stylist's day on the phone

1. Open the app: today's column, your clients, the reminder marks.
2. Before each client, tap the booking: allergies, patch test, last formula, form answers.
3. Press **Arrived**, **Start**, **Done** as the visit goes.
4. Add the formula as a note. Take a before and after photo from the client's profile.
5. A push alert tells you about new online bookings for you; tap it to open the booking.
