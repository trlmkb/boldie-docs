## About this guide

Every screenshot was taken from a test copy of Boldie running a demo salon ("Boldie Demo", three staff: Rūta, Ieva
and Greta). Names, phone numbers and prices in the pictures are demo data. Last checked against the product on
29 October 2026.

:::note[Before launch]
Texts, emails, payments and push notifications were not connected to live providers when this guide was written.
Screens that depend on them are shown as they behave on a test server (for example, the billing pages say
"Test mode"). Where a step needs a live provider, the guide says so.
:::
