The apps are for staff on the floor. Desk work (reports, marketing, settings, team, billing, editing services, import)
stays on the web.

| iPhone | Android |
|---|---|
| ![iPhone calendar](../../assets/ios/calendar-light.png) | ![Android calendar](../../assets/android/calendar-light.png) |

## Sign in

Enter your **Email** and **Password** and tap **Sign in**. If you belong to several salons, pick one. Allow
notifications when asked, so you get alerts.

![Android sign-in](../../assets/android/sign-in-light.png)

## Tabs

**Calendar**, **Online** (with a badge counting requests), **Clients**, **Services**.

## Calendar on the phone

- **Day** shows every column you can see; swipe sideways for more. **Week** shows one person's week (pick whose).
- Arrows and **Today** move between days.
- The status at the top says **Live**, **Syncing…** or **Offline**. The calendar is saved on the phone, so it opens
  without a connection; changes need one. On Android, the ↻ button refreshes; on iPhone the calendar refreshes when you
  open the app.
- **Move a booking:** press and hold it, then drag. **Change the length:** drag the bottom edge. If the new time is
  taken, the booking springs back with a message.
- The marks and badges are the same as on the web ([The calendar](/calendar/#reading-the-calendar)).

## Book or block from the phone

Tap an empty time.

- **Booking:** search the client (or **New client**, or leave empty for a walk-in), pick the service, tap **Book**. On
  iPhone, booking outside working hours asks **Book anyway**.
- **Block:** pick the kind, length and reason. Turn on **Repeat weekly** for a repeating block.

| iPhone: new booking | Android: new booking | Android: new block |
|---|---|---|
| ![iPhone new booking](../../assets/ios/new-booking-search-light.png) | ![Android new booking](../../assets/android/new-booking-light.png) | ![Android block](../../assets/android/new-block-light.png) |

Tap a repeating block for **Skip this one**, **Delete this and following** or **Delete series**. One-off blocks are
removed on the web.

![iPhone repeating block](../../assets/ios/repeating-block-light.png)

## The booking sheet on the phone

Tap a booking. You see the phone number (tap to call), warnings (allergies, patch test, no-shows), the service, time and
price, and the status buttons with the same rules as on the web. Below: forms with **Fill in now**, notes (**Visit**,
**Formula**, General on Android) and **Cancel booking**. Pending online bookings have **Approve** and **Decline**.

## Online

**Requests** lists online bookings awaiting approval: **Approve** or **Decline**. **Waitlist** shows who is waiting and
their state (read-only; add people on the web). **Show closed entries** includes booked and expired ones.

| Android requests | Android waitlist | iPhone requests (phase 4 run) |
|---|---|---|
| ![Requests](../../assets/android/online-requests-light.png) | ![Waitlist](../../assets/android/waitlist-light.png) | ![iPhone requests](../../assets/qa/phase-4/ios/22-02-requests-light.png) |

## Clients on the phone

Search by name, phone or email; tap **+** (iPhone) or **New client** (Android) to add one. A profile has contact
details, warnings, details, consent, forms, patch tests, notes, photos (from the library or the camera) and history.
Android shows these as tabs; iPhone shows them in one scrolling page.

## Salon menu and calendar subscription

iPhone: the building icon at the top left of Calendar. Android: tap the salon name. The menu switches salons, opens
**Calendar subscription** (create the link, add it to Apple or Google Calendar, copy or share it) and has **Sign out**.
