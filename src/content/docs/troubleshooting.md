---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Common problems and what to do."
---

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No salon yet" after signing in | Your account is not in a salon. Ask the owner to invite you, or contact Boldie to set up your salon |
| The app shows "No salons" | Same as above. The salon is created on the web side first |
| A booking springs back after a drag | The new time clashes with another booking, a block, the stylist's hours or a busy chair. The message says which |
| "Book anyway" appears | The time is outside the stylist's working hours. Book anyway only if they agreed to stay |
| Calendar says Offline | You are seeing the saved copy. Changes need a connection; reconnect and it says Live again |
| A red ! on a booking | The reminder text failed. Check **Messages → Delivery log**, fix the number, **Retry**, or phone the client |
| Clients don't receive texts | Check the client has a mobile number and has not replied STOP; check the delivery log; check quiet hours |
| Online booking won't switch on | On a live server the SMS service must be set up, because clients confirm their phone with a code |
| A client can't see a service online | Check **Bookable online** on the service, **Services online** in Settings, and that someone who performs it is online |
| No good times on the booking page | Check working hours, lead time, "bookable up to", and each staff member's **Min. gap** |
| Google Calendar is behind | Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, sometimes hours. The Boldie app is always current |
| No push alerts on the phone | Allow notifications for Boldie in the phone's settings; check **Alerts to staff → Push** |
